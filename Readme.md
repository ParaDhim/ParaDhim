### 🔬 Research & Engineering Focus

I am an ML researcher and systems engineer working at the intersection of **empirical machine learning, applied cryptography, generative model safety, and clinical NLP**:

- **Generative AI Safety**: Decoding-time speculative interventions in text-to-image diffusion trajectories without modifying base model weights (*GuardPaint*).
- **Empirical ML & Neural Cryptanalysis**: Cipher-agnostic deep architectures, ablation benchmarks, and statistical evaluation rigor (*NeuroCrypt*).
- **Production Systems & Clinical NLP**: High-throughput distributed NLP pipelines, OMOP CDM longitudinal knowledge graphs, and HIPAA-compliant de-identification engines.
